**Description**

“Deepfake” (from deep learning and fake) is AI-generated content in which a person in digital media is partly or completely replaced by another. GenAI advances allow individuals to appear saying or doing things that never occurred, blurring the line between authentic and synthetic.

High-accuracy AI models trained on labeled real and synthetic examples can detect and flag modified media.

Detecting deepfakes benefits (1) people whose likeness is misused, (2) social media platforms in discerning AI-generated content from authentic content, (3) lawmakers and legal experts, and (4) corporations guarding their brands.

**Results**

The final validation accuracy of the CNN deepfake detection model, trained using >4,000 image data and with 20 epochs, is 80.43 percent. Refer to [Results](https://github.com/Rodin-Sh/DeepfakeDetectionModel/blob/main/Results.csv) for full accuracy (%) data per epoch.

**Dataset**

In this project, [Deepfake Detection Challenge](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/deepfake-detection-challenge/data) dataset was utilized to train and validate the accuracy of the CNN deepfake detection model.
